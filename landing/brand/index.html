Brand guidelines

One word. One point.
One red o.

Everything about Mono comes from the reader: a single word, held still on a reticle, its focus letter in red. The logo, the icon and every page are built from that one picture.

The idea

The focus point

  1. The red oIn the reader, the letter your eye lands on is red. In the name, the o plays that part: always red, always the first o.
  2. The reticleTicks above and below the focus letter, arms on the centre line. Every mark fades as it nears the word.
  3. Still, not busyOne word, lots of black around it. Space is part of the mark.

Logo

The wordmark

Drawn, not typed: four letters of round-capped strokes, the first o heavier and red. Use white on dark backgrounds and black on light ones; the o stays red on both.

Icon

The wordmark, up close

The app icon crops the wordmark around its red o and puts it on the reticle, as if the reader had just stopped on it. At favicon sizes only the o and its ticks remain.

Colour

Black, white and one red

Red marks the point of focus — the o, the focus letter, the one action that matters on a screen — and nothing else. Everything around it is black, white and the system's greys.

Voice

Calm, plain, brief

Mono talks the way it reads: one thing at a time. Short sentences, everyday words, no hype and no numbers we can't stand behind.

Do

  • Say what happens: “Your place is saved as you read.”
  • Speak to one reader, as “you”.
  • Let red mark one thing per screen.
  • Leave room around the logo: at least the height of the o on every side.

Don't

  • Promise results: no “read 3× faster”, no made-up statistics.
  • Recolour, outline, stretch or add effects to the wordmark.
  • Move the red to another letter, or make the whole word red.
  • Set “Mono” in a font in place of the drawn wordmark.
