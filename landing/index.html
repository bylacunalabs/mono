How it works

The words come to you.

Reading a page, your eyes hop from word to word and line to line, and every hop costs time. Mono uses RSVP — Rapid Serial Visual Presentation — to put each word in the same place, so your eyes never have to move.

On a page

Dozens of jumps per line, a sweep back at every line's end, and the odd look back to find your place.

With Mono

One spot. The red letter is the optimal recognition point, just left of the word's middle, where your gaze naturally lands — and it is always in the centre of the reticle.

Features

Built for reading whole books.

A rhythm that feels natural, any text you have, and a library that remembers where you were.

A rhythm that reads like you do

Sentences end with a short pause, frequent words pass quicker and long ones linger. Very long words are split into syllables. The speed you set is the real average, pauses included.

Bring any text

Import books, documents and saved pages, paste text, drop files on the library or share them to Mono.

One gesture away

Tap to pause and see the whole text. Swipe to skip by sentence, paragraph or chapter, up or down for speed, pinch for size. Or use Space and the arrow keys.

Never lose the thread

Your place is saved as you read. After a pause Mono picks up a few words back and eases into speed, so you're straight back in.

A library with a memory

Covers, progress and time left for every text, search, the words you read today and your daily streak.

Private and offline

No account, no server: your texts and statistics are stored on your device and Mono works without a connection. Back up everything to a file whenever you like.

At home on your phone

Feels like an app. Because it is one.

Add Mono to your Home Screen and it opens full screen, follows your system's look on iPhone and Android, light or dark, and reads in your choice of seven typefaces.

The library, and the reader paused on the whole text.

Install

Free, and nothing to download from a store.

Mono runs in your browser. Install it to get an icon, full screen and offline reading.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open Mono in Safari.
  2. Tap Share.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.

Android

  1. Open Mono in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install when Mono offers it, or open the menu.
  3. Choose Install app.

Computer

  1. Open Mono in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the install icon in the address bar.
  3. Open EPUBs and PDFs with Mono from your files.
